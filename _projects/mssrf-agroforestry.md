## The question

Native trees in and around rice fields could support birds that eat crop pests. If so, conserving those trees is part of sustainable agriculture, not a cost to it.

## My work

- Field surveys across nine agroforestry sites in rice-based agroecosystems in Wayanad, recording 128 plant species and 101 bird species.
- Biodiversity census, field data collection and data management.
- Statistical analysis and ecological modelling in R, linking biodiversity to pest control.
- Contributed to drafting the manuscript.
- Took part in interactive sessions on bird survey methods and with tribal communities, whose knowledge of local trees and land use is part of how these agroecosystems are managed.

## Publication

Lopus M, et al., Shafi S (2023). Conservation of native tree species in the agroforest of rice-based agroecosystems will contribute to sustainable agriculture. *Biodiversity and Conservation*. [doi:10.1007/s10531-023-02738-0](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10531-023-02738-0)

## What I took from it

Field ecology taught me to design sampling before collecting data, and to be careful about what a correlation across sites can and cannot show. The same caution applies when linking environmental resistance genes to their sources.
