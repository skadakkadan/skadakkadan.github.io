## The programme

Gene Editors of the Future is run by the Genome Engineering Laboratory at the University of Westminster, created and led by Dr Kalpana Surendranath.

- **Gene Editors of the Future 2.0** (October 2022 – January 2023): online research and training on the basics of CRISPR/Cas9 human genome engineering.
- **Gene Editors of the Future** (2023): practical CRISPR-Cas9 training, alongside core molecular biology techniques: qPCR, electrophoresis, cloning and Western blotting.

## Outreach at the Royal Institution

In 2023 I represented the programme at a Genetics Society outreach event at the Royal Institution in London. With other members of the programme I presented a poster and talked with members of the public about CRISPR: where it came from in bacterial immunity, how it is used today, and what it could do next.
