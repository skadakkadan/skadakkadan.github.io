How I grade evidence
{% include grade.html level="strong" %}

Claim follows directly from well-controlled data, ideally confirmed by an independent method.

{% include grade.html level="moderate" %}

Data support the claim, but a key control, replicate or orthogonal method is missing.

{% include grade.html level="weak" %}

Claim goes beyond the data, or relies on correlation where causation is stated.

{% include grade.html level="preliminary" %}

Interesting but early: preprint, small n, or a single experiment.
